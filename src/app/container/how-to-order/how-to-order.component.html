Our Process

Steps to Place Your Order

Ordering custom apparel has never been easier. At Unique Prints, we streamline the entire process to make your experience hassle-free. Follow our step-by-step guide and let us handle the rest.

Pick Your Apparel

Browse our collection of apparel options and select your desired style, color, size, and quantity. If you need assistance, our team is here to help you choose the perfect fit for your needs.

Request a Custom Quote

Provide your details through our quote form. Whether you have precise information or need a quick estimate, we've got you covered with tailored options for every situation.

Submit Your Design

Upload your artwork directly through our site. If you're unsure about the format, our team will assist in optimizing your design. We accept multiple file types and offer guidance for the best print quality.

Our Commitment

What Happens Next

Once we receive your details, our dedicated team jumps into action to ensure your order is handled swiftly and professionally.

Design Preview

We create a digital mockup of your design on the selected apparel, allowing you to visualize the final product before proceeding.

Detailed Quote

Based on your specifications, we provide a transparent and competitive quote to help you make an informed decision.

Sample Option

Need a physical sample? We can provide a prototype for a nominal fee to ensure you're satisfied with the quality before full production.

Production

Upon your approval, we initiate the printing process. Our team ensures that every piece meets our high standards of quality.

Delivery

Once printed, we meticulously pack your order and coordinate with reliable couriers to deliver your custom apparel right to your doorstep.
